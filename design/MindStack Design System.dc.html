06

Motion

One curve for everything. Three showpiece moments; everywhere else the motion should be barely noticed.

Tokens
ease  cubic-bezier(.22,.61,.36,1)
hover  120ms
reveal  400ms · 14px rise
stagger  60ms
page   240ms fade
showpiece  ≤1600ms, once
Rules
Reveals fire once, never on scroll-up. Nothing animates in forms or on Contact. No parallax on text. No scroll-jacking on touch — sticky sequences become stacked cards below 900px. prefers-reduced-motion removes every reveal and freezes showpieces on their final frame.
07

Imagery & icons

Image direction
PLACEHOLDER PATTERN
USED UNTIL REAL ASSETS ARRIVE
Product interfaces at 16:10, shown square-on and cropped to one meaningful region — never tilted in a laptop mockup. Team photography only if it's real; if there is none, the site stays typographic. No stock imagery, no illustration, no 3D renders.
Icons
1.5px stroke, 24px grid, square caps, no fills, no rounded corners beyond 2px. Geometric and dry, drawn from the same vocabulary as the mark. Icons label, they don't decorate — no icon per bullet point.
